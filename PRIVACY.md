# Privacy Policy — Engram

**Last updated:** 11 August 2026

Engram is an offline note and spaced-repetition app. This policy explains what
the app does with your data. It is short because the app does very little.

## What Engram collects

**Nothing leaves your device.** Engram has no account system, no server, no
analytics, no advertising SDK and no crash reporting. It does not make network
requests.

Everything you capture — text, photos, video, audio recordings, PDF excerpts,
your review history and your settings — is stored in the app's private storage
on your phone and nowhere else.

## Permissions and why they are asked

Every permission is requested **in context**, at the moment you first use the
feature that needs it — never at launch. Declining any of them leaves the rest
of the app fully usable.

| Permission | Used for |
|---|---|
| Camera | Taking a photo or recording a video for a card. |
| Microphone | Recording a voice note for a card, and audio while recording video. |
| Photos and videos | Showing your recent photos and videos so you can turn one into a card. |
| Notifications | Sending the daily reminder you turn on in Settings. |
| Alarms and reminders | Delivering that reminder at the time you chose. |

Captured media is copied into the app's own storage. Engram does not modify or
delete anything in your gallery.

## Where your data goes when you export it

Settings › Export data writes your cards, review history, settings and media
files to a folder **you** choose. From that point the files are yours and this
policy no longer governs them — if you place that folder in a cloud drive, that
provider's terms apply.

## Deleting your data

Settings › Delete all data removes every card, every review record, every media
file and your settings from the device. It cannot be undone. Uninstalling the
app also removes everything.

## Children

Engram is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal
information from anyone.
